## Research

The story-level guidance draws on [StoryScope: Investigating idiosyncrasies in AI fiction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03136v1) by Jenna Russell, Rishanth Rajendhran, Mohit Iyyer, and John Wieting (preprint), and on Wikipedia's community guide, [Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing).
